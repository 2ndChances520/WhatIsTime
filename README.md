## 🚀 Project Overview

**What Is Time?** is an interactive digital experience that explores the profound intersection between modern cosmological physics and ancient theological truths. This project serves as a visual and mathematical summary of time—from the infinitesimal **Planck Epoch** to the theoretical **Final Collapse**.

Through the lens of "The Master Builder," this webpage bridges the gap between Einstein's curvature of spacetime and the foundational narratives of Genesis.

## 🌌 The Journey Through Time

The webpage is structured into five distinct explorations:

### 1. The Foundation (Genesis)
Exploring the "Blueprint." How the laws of physics were established before the formation of matter.

### 2. The Mystery of Time
A look at the paradox of time in Quantum Mechanics vs. General Relativity. Why can we measure time to 18 decimal places, yet still struggle to define its essence?

### 3. The Precision of Creation
How God's design is revealed through the **Cesium-133 atom**. Exploring the exact frequency ($9,192,631,770$ cycles/sec) that defines our very reality.

### 4. Dimensionality & M-Theory
Moving beyond the 3rd dimension into the **5th Dimension** and higher-order mathematics (Kaluza-Klein theory), where time and space converge into a single geometry.

### 5. Gravitational Dilation
Understanding gravity not just as a force, but as the literal warping of the fabric of spacetime—where massive objects slow the very ticking of the clock.
